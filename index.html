How to play

🎯 Goal. Walk to each glowing portal (1–6), solve the puzzle inside, and pick up the artifact. Bring all 6 to the Codex pedestal in the middle of the hub to win.

🕹 Move. Arrow keys or W A S D on a keyboard. The on-screen D-pad on touch.

👉 Interact / continue. Space or Enter on a keyboard. The yellow A button on touch.

⏸ Pause. Esc, or the ⏸ Pause button. Esc also closes any open dialog.

🔇 Sound. Off by default. Tap the speaker icon in the HUD to toggle.

💡 Hint & Skip. Inside any puzzle, tap Hint for a nudge. After 30 seconds you can also Skip — kids never get stuck.

❓ Where to look. Walk up to glowing portals, signs, robots, blackboards, vacuum tubes, and oddities — then press Space.
